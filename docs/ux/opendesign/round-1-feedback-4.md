The approval card is approved as it is. Now the editor around it: it reads like a CRM — a row of eight text buttons in the top bar, a boxed header on every panel, borders everywhere. Forma is a design tool where a person and an agent work on a piece; it should feel like a calm canvas with tools that appear when needed. Add this as a second layout, switchable in the review rail ("Layout: Current / Calm"), keeping every scenario and state working in both, and the approval card unchanged.

Direction for "Calm" (adapt, don't copy any product):

1. **The canvas is the room.** It gets almost all the space; regions are separated by tone (Ink / Surface), not by boxed borders. Panel titles become small quiet labels or disappear where the content is obvious.
2. **Top bar shrinks to essentials:** left — Forma symbol, "Projects /" breadcrumb, design name and a quiet version tag; right — Undo/Redo as icon buttons, a validation status chip ("✓ Valid" or "2 issues", opens a popover with the list), and **Export as the one primary action**. Nothing else up there.
3. **Left: one slim icon rail** (Layers & pages, Assets, Brand) with tooltips; only one flyout open at a time, Layers open by default; pages as a compact switcher at the top of the layers flyout.
4. **Properties is contextual, not a toggle:** it appears on the right only when something is selected, and goes away when nothing is.
5. **Zoom moves to a small floating control at the bottom-right of the canvas** ("− 100% +").
6. **The agent is a presence, not a panel among panels.** Collapsed: a floating composer at the bottom-center of the canvas ("Ask the agent…") with a small status dot. Expanded: the chat docks on the right as it does today (status header, transcript, composer). An approval arriving while collapsed expands the dock, or shows a compact bubble above the floating composer with the card's sentence, the countdown and "Review".
7. Fewer visible controls at rest, nothing lost: every action that existed is still reachable within one click or a shortcut, and every icon button has a tooltip and an accessible name.
8. Same design system: Ink, warm neutrals, Geist, Signal only for active/selected/primary.

After building it, test both layouts across every scenario and state yourself and fix what breaks.
