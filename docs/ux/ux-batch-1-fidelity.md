**Audit:** 2026-10-06, rebased on `origin/main` at `a59c0e7`. Prototype and app viewports were 1595×937. The prototype copies were served from `/tmp/forma-ux1-fidelity/prototypes` with the app's Geist and Geist Mono WOFF2 files in `assets/`. Each prototype was opened in an Orca browser tab; app evidence is from FakeProvider instance 33. Prototype screenshots were captured at the matching viewport after Orca's screenshot command timed out on the prototype tab. App screenshots were captured in Orca. Both used Geist and Geist Mono; no font-family substitution was observed.

The prototypes' review rails sit outside their simulated app frame. The build does not include those rails or their fake data, per C-6; the old layout switch is also absent per C-4. Rows that say **not reproducible on FakeProvider** use the closest actual build capture for the page plus the named implementation/tests for state comparison. They do not claim that the adjacent app screenshot displays the prototype's synthetic state.
