## PV-1 blockers

- The visual loop and `pv1_visual_ready` remain blocked on Pontis CR-2.3; S5 cannot pass until the platform can deliver image results to the model.
- Long-session acceptance remains blocked by Pontis delegation expiry: the active session loses authority after about five minutes and cannot refresh it. See [F-30-5](pontis-browser-findings/30-approvals.md#f-30-5--a-chat-session-dies-about-5-minutes-after-it-starts-and-nothing-lets-the-person-start-another) and the separate real-Codex notice gap, [F-30-10](pontis-browser-findings/30-approvals.md#f-30-10--on-real-codex-an-expired-session-leaves-no-notice-and-no-new-session-button). Step 12 does not claim either is fixed.
